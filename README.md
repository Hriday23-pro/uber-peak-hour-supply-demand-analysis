# Uber Peak Hour Supply–Demand Analysis

A business analyst case study on driver availability, unserved rider demand, and the cost of targeted incentives. The analysis uses five weeks of hourly rider and driver data (14 November–18 December 2016).

## Key findings

- Riders could not find a car during 19 recurring hours of a typical week, when drivers were already busy.
- Reaching an 85% rider coverage target would require about 164 additional driving hours per week in those periods.
- At a €12 hourly guarantee, topping up only the incremental hours is estimated at about €719 per week. Extending the guarantee to all drivers in those periods would cost about €2,719 per week.
- The report recommends shifting idle driver hours first, testing targeted morning incentives, and treating late-night shortages as a possible positioning issue.

These are estimates based on the case data and stated assumptions, not a validated operating forecast. The report explains the assumptions and data limitations.
